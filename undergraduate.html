B.S. in Information Systems

The IS major prepares students to analyze, design, and build information systems that solve organizational problems while considering people, processes, and technology.

Sample Curriculum Highlights

  • IS 147 — Introduction to Computer Programming
  • IS 247 — Computer Programming II
  • IS 300 — Management Information Systems
  • IS 310 — Software and Hardware Concepts
  • IS 436 — Structured Systems Analysis & Design

Skills You’ll Build

  • Systems analysis & requirements gathering
  • Data modeling and SQL
  • Web/application development fundamentals
  • Teamwork, communication, documentation

Outcomes & Opportunities

Graduates pursue roles such as Business Analyst, Systems Analyst, Data Analyst, and IT Project Coordinator in government, healthcare, finance, and technology sectors.
